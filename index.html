About Us

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). Headquartered in Chhattisgarh and operating across India, the foundation is dedicated to creating lasting social impact through education, social awareness, youth empowerment, and community development. With the support of a strong network of professionals and volunteers, InAmigos Foundation strives to bring positive change to society through meaningful projects, awareness initiatives, and community-driven efforts.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project SEVA - Food & Clothing Distribution

Helps underprivileged families, daily wage workers, and homeless people by providing meals and clothes. The foundation mentions distributing 50,000+ meals.

Project BACHPANSHALA - Education for Children

Focuses on educating underprivileged children through learning support, digital literacy, school assistance, and educational resources.

Project JEEV - Animal Welfare

Works for stray animal feeding, rescue, protection, and medical care. Volunteers reportedly feed 50+ stray animals daily.

Project UDAAN - Women Empowerment

Supports women through skill development, financial literacy, self-help groups, hygiene awareness, and entrepreneurship training.

Project PRAKRITI - Environmental Sustainability

Organizes tree plantation drives, environmental awareness campaigns, clean-up initiatives, and promotes eco-friendly practices. The foundation mentions planting 20,000+ saplings.

Project VIKAS - Skill Development and Internships

Provides internship and skill-building opportunities in areas like digital marketing, research, finance, content writing, social work, and technology to improve employability among youth.
